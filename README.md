# Lamhti Privacy Policy
**Last updated: May 2026**

## Data We Collect
- Email address (for account and notifications)
- Photos you upload for sale
- Payment transaction records (via Apple IAP / Stripe)
- Device info for app functionality

## How We Use Your Data
- To provide marketplace services
- To send purchase confirmation emails
- To moderate uploaded content

## Third-Party Services
- **Firebase** — authentication and storage
- **Apple In-App Purchase** — iOS payments
- **Stripe** — Android payments  
- **HuggingFace** — image content moderation

## Data Sharing
We do not sell your personal data to third parties.
